# Registration and signatories

The GSA is a **Registered Student Organization (RSO)** at UC Berkeley. That
registration lets us book rooms, hold campus money and run events, and it
expires every year. This page describes how it works. The
[officers page](officers.md) lists who holds each role this year.

## What being an RSO means

Berkeley recognises us so that we can use campus facilities, funding and
services. Recognition does not make us part of the University.

- We are a **separate legal entity** from UC Berkeley, and we are responsible
  for the safety and administration of everything we run, on campus or off.
- Recognition covers **on-campus** activity only.
- People who attend our events participate **voluntarily and at their own risk**.
- We may describe an event as University-sponsored or endorsed only with
  permission from an authorised University official.

For this reason, a disclaimer appears in the footer of every page on this site.
Berkeley requires the exact wording; see
[naming, logos and this website](#naming-logos-and-this-website).

## The annual cycle

**Berkeley clears all signatory statuses on 30 June.** No status carries over.
Each year every signatory completes the LEAD Center's Signatory Training bCourse
and establishes the role again.

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| 1 | Each of the four signatories completes the **Signatory Training bCourse** individually |
| 2 | In the course's final module, you list the org and its **CalLink org ID** |
| 3 | You agree to the CalLink Signatory Terms and Conditions |
| 4 | OASIS adds the signatory position to the org's CalLink roster, usually **within 5 business days**. OASIS processes signatories weekly |
| 5 | **You accept the position in CalLink.** Acceptance is a separate step, so watch for the email |
| 6 | Room-booking rights appear in 25Live **within about 48 hours** of the roster upload |

!!! warning "Allow a week before anyone can book a room"
    Finishing the course and gaining working 25Live access takes **up to seven
    business days**. Until then nobody can reserve space or request money for the
    org, because OASIS rejects requests from pending orgs and from
    non-signatories. We recommend completing it over the summer.

Certified signatories alone can reserve campus space, apply for funding and
manage the org's CalLink presence. The org itself also needs **active status in
CalLink**, which is a separate annual registration.

## Signatory status and financial agent status differ

Signatory status covers reservations. Spending money through CalLink requires a
second status, **ASUC/GA Agent User, Stage 2**, which comes from a second
bCourse: the CalLink Finance Agent Training, linked from the CalLink homepage.

!!! warning "Two separate trainings, both cleared every summer"
    Signatory training does not confer agent status. Berkeley removes agent
    status granted in previous years each summer, along with everything else.
    OASIS recommends that the org keep **at least two financial agents** at all
    times. Agents approve purchase requests, so one person graduating can
    otherwise leave the org unable to spend.

## What the constitution must contain

Berkeley requires two statements verbatim in every RSO constitution: a
**non-discrimination statement** covering membership, and an **anti-hazing
statement**. Claiming ASUC money adds a third, the ASUC clause. See
[funding events](funding-events.md#asuc-money-requires-sponsorship).

## Naming, logos and this website

The University owns its name and marks, and enforces this on student groups
closely.

- University marks may not appear in an org's name. The permitted forms are
  "… **at** Berkeley", "… **of** Berkeley", "… at California" and
  "… of California". "Berkeley", "Cal", "UC", "UCB" and "Golden Bear" may not
  form part of a name, and an org may use one designation only.
- **Bear and paw imagery is prohibited everywhere**, including a CalLink profile
  picture and a social media avatar, and including original artwork that uses no
  University mark. Brand Management rarely approves AI-generated art.
- Outside the `berkeley.edu` domain, keep University names and abbreviations out
  of URLs, handles and profile pictures. Our OCF subdomain,
  `cph.studentorg.berkeley.edu`, is inside the domain and is fine.
- Plain unstyled text using "UC Berkeley" factually in prose, as this site does,
  is permitted.
- Merchandise goes through an approved licensee, and campus policy prohibits
  **selling products on campus**. Licensee lists and turnaround times change, so
  ask `brandmanagement@berkeley.edu`.

!!! note "Berkeley requires the footer disclaimer"
    Every page of an RSO website must carry this sentence in visible readable
    text: "We are a student group acting independently of the University of
    California, Berkeley. We take full responsibility for our organization and
    this website." It is set once in `mkdocs.yml` under `copyright` and renders
    site-wide. Keep it there.

Sponsor logos follow their own rule. When a campus department co-sponsors, its
name goes **first and alone**, clearly separated, with other sponsors smaller and
below. Every logo use needs written permission, the department's included.

## Member data

Get **explicit consent** before sharing members' names, email addresses or
membership in the org with anyone outside it. The same applies to lists
circulated among members. Collect as little as you can.

If anyone asks the GSA for student data, talk to the OASIS advisor before you
reply. The site's own rules on
[what stays unpublished](../index.md#this-site-is-public) cover the rest.

!!! note "Your advisor reports SVSH disclosures"
