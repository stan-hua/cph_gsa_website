## Why bother

Written bylaws matter for exactly two moments: a contested election, and a
disagreement about money. Both are rare and both go badly without a written rule
to point at.

A minimal set covers:

- Who is a member (everyone in the program)
- What officers exist and what each does
- How and when elections happen, and who can vote
- How the budget is approved and who can spend
- How the bylaws themselves get amended
