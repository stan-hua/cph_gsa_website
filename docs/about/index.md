# About the GSA

## What we do

The Graduate Student Association represents students in the joint UC Berkeley–UCSF
PhD program to both campuses' administrations, and maintains this site.

The official program handbook calls the GSA "a platform for advocacy,
community-building, and collaboration" between students, faculty and
administration, and lists organizing events, providing peer support, and
facilitating communication between students, faculty and administration.

## Money for student events

CPH runs a **Graduate Student Community/Events Fund**, and any CPH student can
propose an event or a purchase for the student community. Talk to the GSAO, submit
an Expense Request Form, and wait for approval before spending. See
[funding events](funding-events.md).

## The Leadership and Community Engagement Fellowship

Students in **year 2 or beyond** can apply for the CPH Student Leadership and
Community Engagement Fellowship, a paid year-long appointment with a monthly
stipend from August to May. Applications open each spring, and fellows hear back
in late summer. Ask the GSAO.

## Keeping the org registered

Berkeley recognises us as a Registered Student Organization, and that recognition
needs renewing every year. See
[registration and signatories](registration-and-signatories.md), and
[planning an event](../events/planning-an-event.md) before running anything.

## How to get involved

Every student in the program is a member, with no membership step. Officers are
elected each spring and serve one year. Come to a [meeting](meetings.md).
