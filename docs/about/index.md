# About the GSA

## What we do

The Graduate Student Association represents students in the joint UC Berkeley–UCSF
PhD program to both campuses' administrations, runs the program's social and
professional events, and maintains this site.

Concretely, that means:

- Bringing student concerns to program leadership, with the weight of a body
  rather than an individual
- Running orientation, socials, and the annual retreat
- Administering a small budget for student-proposed events
- Keeping the handbook on this site current

## How to get involved

Every student in the program is a member. Officers are elected each spring and
serve one year. Come to a [meeting](meetings.md) — there's no membership step.
