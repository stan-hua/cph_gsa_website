# Your first semester

## Getting registered

In year 1 you register at **UC Berkeley**, unless you were admitted directly to a
lab at UCSF.

Registration requires two things:

1. Enrolment in **at least one class**, and
2. **At least 20% of tuition and fees** paid by the due date.

Enrol through **CalCentral** at UC Berkeley, which has a *CalCentral: Course
Enrollment* video covering the steps, and through the **Student Portal** at UCSF.

!!! warning "A hold blocks registration"
    An active hold on your account blocks registration completely, and it stays
    until someone clears it. Contact the GSAO.

!!! tip "Book a GSAO meeting in week 1"
    The handbook encourages this. Use it to confirm your fellowship or appointment
    tuition is disbursing, check your class schedule, and ask whatever else has
    come up.

## What you're enrolled in

Every term you need **12 semester units or 8 quarter units**, counting required
courses, electives and research units together.

Your first year is mostly fixed:

| Course | What | Campus |
|---|---|---|
| CPH 200A / 200B / 200C | Cornerstone series: mandatory, CPH students only | UCSF |
| CPH 270 | CPH Doctoral Seminar (two quarters of it must fall in year 1) | UCB or UCSF |
| GRAD 202 | Race and Racism in Science | UCSF, fall quarter |
| GRAD 214 / RCR | Responsible conduct of research | UCSF |

Plus electives chosen with your academic advisor. CPH students get **priority
registration** for a set of UC Berkeley courses in year 1. Full list on the
[curriculum page](../academics/curriculum.md#electives).

## Before summer: California residency

US citizens are **expected to establish California residency after the first
year**. You submit the documents through CalCentral and the Student Portal the
summer *before* year 2.

Skipping this paperwork means paying non-resident tuition. You have to satisfy the
"physical presence" and "intent to remain in California" requirements by the
residence determination date, which is the first day of instruction. Raise
questions with the GSAO early.

International students cannot establish residency and instead pay **Non-Resident
Supplemental Tuition (NRST)** each semester. Filing taxes also works differently;
see [taxes](../academics/taxes.md). CPH may cover NRST for up to two
years, as set out in your offer letter, and the Graduate Division waives it for up
to three calendar years after you advance to candidacy. This is one reason the
[qual deadline](../academics/quals.md#timing-and-eligibility) is 15 July of year 2.

## If you are doing rotations

Not everyone rotates. Students admitted directly to a lab work with their primary
advisor from the start, and this section applies to the rest.

**Rotation 1 is assigned before fall semester starts**, by mutual agreement
between you and the faculty member. A rotation runs a semester or a quarter rather
than a few weeks.

If you and your rotation 1 host both decide against continuing, the advising team
helps you find rotations 2 and 3. CPH funds **up to three**. See
[choosing an advisor](../academics/advisors.md#rotations).
