# Before you arrive

Everything on this page can be done from wherever you are now, and doing it early
saves a genuinely stressful first week.

## Accounts and access

You will end up with two identities. Set both up before you need them.

- **UC Berkeley CalNet ID** — gates bCourses, campus wifi, library access, and most
  UC Berkeley forms.
- **UCSF MyAccess** — gates UCSF email, APeX training if your work touches clinical
  data, and UCSF library resources.

Pick one address as your working address and forward the other to it. Most people
regret splitting their mail across both for a year.

!!! tip
    Set up two-factor on both accounts now, on a phone you'll still have in
    September. Recovering a locked account across two institutions is slow.

## Paperwork

- I-9 and payroll setup at whichever campus is paying you
- Health insurance enrolment (see [wellness](../life/wellness.md))
- International students: visa documents, SEVIS, and arrival check-in
- Any background or immunization requirements if your research is clinical

## Things worth doing that nobody tells you

- **Email a few current students.** The GSA can connect you. Ten minutes with a
  third-year is worth more than any page on this site.
- **Read two or three papers from labs you might rotate in.** Rotation
  conversations go better when you have a question rather than an interest.
- **Sort out a bank account and phone plan early** if you're moving from abroad;
  both are needed for a lease.
