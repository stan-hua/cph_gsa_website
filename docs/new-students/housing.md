# Finding housing

You will spend time in both Berkeley and San Francisco, and no neighborhood is
convenient for both.

## The core tradeoff

Living near your home campus makes daily life easy and the other campus a long
trip. Living in between, which means the East Bay along BART, makes both commutes
moderate.
