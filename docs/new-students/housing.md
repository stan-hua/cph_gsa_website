# Finding housing

The hardest logistics problem in a two-campus program: you'll spend time in both
Berkeley and San Francisco, and no neighborhood is convenient for both.

## The core tradeoff

Living near your home campus makes daily life easy and the other campus a real
trip. Living somewhere in between — the Bay's midpoint is roughly the East Bay
along BART — makes both campuses mediocre but neither impossible. Most students
in this program end up choosing based on where their lab is, and moving once.

## Finding roommates

The GSA maintains a housing channel in the student Slack each spring. Incoming
students are added before they arrive — ask the GSA to be added early.

!!! warning "Rental scams target incoming students"
    Never wire a deposit for a place you haven't seen, and be suspicious of any
    landlord who can't meet in person or by video. If a listing seems cheap for
    the neighborhood, it is not real.
