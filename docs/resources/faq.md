??? question "Which campus am I a student at?"
    **In year 1, UC Berkeley.** You matriculate there regardless of where you end
    up, unless you were admitted directly to a lab at UCSF.

    After year 1, you and your faculty advisor settle your **home campus**, and
    registration and enrolment move there.

    You hold accounts on both campuses throughout: a CalNet ID and CalCentral at
    UC Berkeley, MyAccess and the Student Portal at UCSF, plus an ID card for each.

    One consequence surfaces later. **When you advance to candidacy, your primary
    advisor's home campus must match yours.** UC policy requires it, and the
    handbook makes checking it the student's responsibility. See
    [choosing an advisor](../academics/advisors.md#home-campus).

??? question "Can I take courses at the other campus?"
    Yes. The program is built on it. Your required Cornerstone series, practicum,
    GRAD 202 and GRAD 214 are all **at UCSF**, and much of the computational
    coursework is at UC Berkeley. CPH students also get **priority registration**
    for a set of UC Berkeley courses in year 1, with academic advisor approval.

    Berkeley runs semesters and UCSF runs quarters, so your term boundaries
    diverge. Each term you need **12 semester units or 8 quarter units** in total.

    !!! warning "Check both transcripts"
        Courses taken at UC Berkeley reach your UCSF transcript only once the two
        registrars exchange grades, which lags. Review both periodically and raise
        anything missing with the GSAO.

??? question "How long does the program take?"
    **Normative time to degree is five years.** The official expectation: pass the
    qualifying exam by the end of year 2, advance to candidacy immediately after,
    and file the dissertation by the end of year 5.

??? question "When exactly do I have to pass my qual?"
    **By 15 July of your second year.** That deadline exists so non-resident
    supplemental tuition can be waived for the following year.

    Aim earlier. Faculty are largely unavailable over the summer, and the handbook
    encourages finishing before the close of spring semester in year 2. See
    [quals](../academics/quals.md).

??? question "What happens if I want to change advisors?"
    Changing advisors is normal, and the program has a process. Talk to the Head
    Graduate Advisor or the GSAO early. See
    [choosing an advisor](../academics/advisors.md).

??? question "Do I have to teach?"
    **The handbook sets out no required teaching semesters** and describes
    teaching in optional terms throughout. Treat it as something you do for the
    experience or the funding, and check your offer letter, which is the document
    that binds you.

    To teach, tell the GSAO no later than the end of the semester before the one
    you plan to teach in, and budget time for the four first-time GSI requirements
    at UC Berkeley. See
    [curriculum](../academics/curriculum.md#teaching).

??? question "Who do I talk to if something goes wrong with my advisor?"
    Several options, none of which commits you to a formal process:

    - **Head Graduate Advisor**, Maya Petersen, CPH Co-Director. Also serves as
      backup when your own advisor is unavailable.
    - **GSAO**, Matt Malepeai, for anything administrative.
    - **CPH Equity Advisor**, Irene Chen, whose remit includes fairness in how
      students are treated and retained.
    - **Either campus's ombuds office**, confidential and independent.
    - Your GSA president.

??? question "How do I get access to the building after hours?"
    Your **Cal 1 Card** opens buildings at UC Berkeley. At UCSF it is your
    **photo ID card**, which you must display at all times on UCSF premises.
    Request both as soon as your student profiles exist.

    For **BAIR Lab (BWW8)** access, email `bww8-facilities@berkeley.edu` and
    `cvalen@berkeley.edu`, copying your faculty member, who must "reply all" with
    written approval. You then get a form, then access.

??? question "Where do I sit?"
    Shared workspaces at both campuses: the CPH space in the Gateway at Berkeley,
    and 6th-floor cubicles at the Valley Center for Vision at Mission Bay.
    **First come, first served**, with no assignment needed. Clear your desk at the
    end of the day. See
    [working across two campuses](../new-students/two-campuses.md#desks).

??? question "How do I file my taxes?"
    Wages come with a W-2 and withholding; fellowship stipends often arrive with
    neither. International students file on different forms and usually through
    GLACIER first. See [taxes](../academics/taxes.md).
