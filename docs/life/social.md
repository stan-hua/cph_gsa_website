## Wider than CPH

CPH is a small program. Both campuses have much larger graduate communities, and
the handbook encourages joining them — this is where most of the campus-wide
social and professional life actually happens.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Graduate Assembly** (UC Berkeley) | The official representative body for graduate and professional students. Join the mailing list — <https://ga.berkeley.edu/> |
| **Graduate & Professional Student Association** (UCSF) | The UCSF equivalent — <https://gpsa.ucsf.edu/assembly> |
| **Inclusive Excellence Hub** (UC Berkeley) | Campus-wide community space for graduate students |
| **GradPro** (UC Berkeley) | Professional development workshops and one-on-one consultations, academic and non-academic careers |

## Starting something

If you want to run an event, the GSA has a small budget for it and the process is
short: bring it to a monthly meeting. See [meetings](../about/meetings.md).

There's also **CPH's own Graduate Student Community/Events Fund**, open to any CPH
student rather than just GSA officers. The catch is that approval has to come
*before* you spend anything — talk to the GSAO, submit an Expense Request Form,
wait for approval, then buy. See
[funding a student event](../academics/funding.md#funding-a-student-event).

If you find yourself doing a lot of this, the **CPH Student Leadership and
Community Engagement Fellowship** pays a monthly stipend (August–May) for exactly
this kind of work. Open to students in year 2 and beyond; applications each
spring.
