## Wider than CPH

CPH is a small program, and both campuses have much larger graduate communities.
The official handbook encourages joining them.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Graduate Assembly** (UC Berkeley) | The representative body for graduate and professional students. Join the mailing list: <https://ga.berkeley.edu/> |
| **Graduate & Professional Student Association** (UCSF) | The UCSF equivalent: <https://gpsa.ucsf.edu/assembly> |
| **Inclusive Excellence Hub** (UC Berkeley) | Campus-wide community space for graduate students |
| **GradPro** (UC Berkeley) | Professional development workshops and one-on-one consultations, academic and non-academic careers |

## Starting something

**CPH's Graduate Student Community/Events Fund** is open to any CPH student, and
it requires approval *before* you spend anything. See
[funding events](../about/funding-events.md). You can also raise an idea at a GSA
[meeting](../about/meetings.md).

Students doing a lot of this work in year 2 or beyond can apply for the **CPH
Student Leadership and Community Engagement Fellowship**, which pays a monthly
stipend from August to May. Applications open each spring.
