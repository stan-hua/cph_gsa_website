# Living in the Bay Area

Practical notes on the area, beyond what campus orientation covers.

## Getting around

- **BART** connects the East Bay to San Francisco, and most students commute on it.
- **Muni** covers San Francisco itself, including both UCSF campuses.
- **AC Transit** covers the East Bay.
- A **Clipper card** works on all of them, and it can go on your phone.

## Practical setup

- Renters insurance is cheap, and most leases require it. It usually excludes
  earthquake damage
- Earthquake preparedness: keep water and shoes by the bed
- Fire season: get a decent mask, and check AQI before running
