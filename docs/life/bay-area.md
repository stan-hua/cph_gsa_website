# Living in the Bay Area

The parts of moving here that aren't in any official orientation.

## Getting around

- **BART** connects the East Bay to San Francisco and is how most students commute.
- **Muni** covers San Francisco itself, including both UCSF campuses.
- **AC Transit** covers the East Bay.
- A **Clipper card** works on all of them; add it to your phone.

## Practical setup

- Renters insurance is cheap and most leases require it
- Earthquake preparedness: keep water and shoes by the bed, and know that renters
  insurance usually excludes earthquake damage
- Air quality during fire season — get a decent mask and check AQI before running
