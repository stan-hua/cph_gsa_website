## Mental health

Graduate school is hard on people, and using counseling is common rather than
exceptional. Both campuses offer free sessions to enrolled students, and both
have waitlists — so if you think you might want it in a month, book now.

If you are in crisis, call or text **988** (Suicide and Crisis Lifeline), or go to
the nearest emergency room. Both campuses also run 24-hour crisis lines.

## Disability accommodations

Both campuses have disability services offices that arrange accommodations for
coursework, exams and lab work. Registering at one campus does not register you at
the other — you need to do both.
