# Qualifying exam

The qual is the milestone students ask about most and the one about which the
official documentation says least. This page is the students' version.

## Timeline

Working backwards from the exam date:

| When | What |
|---|---|
| 6 months before | Choose committee members, ask them in person |
| 4 months before | Circulate a one-page outline of the proposal |
| 2 months before | Full draft to your advisor |
| 4 weeks before | Draft to the full committee |
| 2 weeks before | Practice talk in front of lab mates. Do this. |
| Exam day | — |

## Choosing a committee

Ask your advisor for suggestions, then ask senior students what those people are
actually like in an exam. Both inputs matter. A committee member who asks hard
questions in a supportive way is worth more than one who asks none.
