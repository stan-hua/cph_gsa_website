# Funding, fellowships and travel

## Where your stipend comes from

CPH offers two kinds of departmental funding: **standard support**, and
**competitive CPH fellowships** awarded at admission or later (ask the GSAO for
current details).

How you're funded depends on how you were admitted.

### If you were Direct Admit

You entered with an explicit commitment from a Type 1 or Type 2 core CPH faculty
member to be your primary advisor and **guarantee full financial support through
year 5 or graduation**, whichever comes first.

| When | Who pays |
|---|---|
| First semester | CPH departmental standard support |
| Second semester of year 1 → year 5 (or graduation) | Your primary advisor, at the minimum stated in your offer letter |

Your advisor's funding also covers tuition, fees, and non-resident supplemental
tuition where applicable. In practice this is usually a **GSR appointment at no
less than 50% FTE**. A GSI appointment, a GSR with different faculty, or an
individual fellowship (an NIH F-award, say) are all acceptable alternatives —
coordinate with your primary advisor.

### If you were not Direct Admit

You entered with at least one Type 1 or Type 2 core CPH faculty member who
offered to host you for a rotation during year 1.

If you and that faculty member both agree after the first rotation, they become
your primary advisor at the end of it. If not, the advising team helps you find
one or two more rotation sites.

| Support | Who gets it | Deferrable? |
|---|---|---|
| First semester standard support | Everyone, DA or not | **Yes** |
| Rotation 2 and 3 support | Non-DA students only | **No** |

Rotation 2 and 3 support exists only to fund those rotations until you have a
primary advisor.

!!! tip "The first semester's support can be banked"
    First-semester standard support may be **deferred to a single future
    semester** — any time within 5 years or until graduation — in consultation
    with your advising team. The amount doesn't shrink by waiting. If your first
    semester is already covered another way, ask the GSAO about deferring rather
    than losing it.

### Topping up

If you hold an external fellowship or a GSR/GSI appointment that pays **less**
than the official support level for your matriculation year, partial departmental
standard support can be used to bring you up to — but not above — the minimum you
were promised. Talk to the GSAO.

## GSR and GSI/TA appointments

These pay a monthly salary and cover tuition and fees for the term.

| Appointment | Hours |
|---|---|
| 50% | 20 hrs/week |
| 25% | 10 hrs/week |

**50% is the cap.** Student academic appointments are limited to half time so you
still have at least half your time for your own degree. Going above 50% in a
semester requires an Exception Request Petition approved by the Head Graduate
Advisor — and **international students are capped at 50% every term, no
exceptions.**

Summers differ: if UC Berkeley is your home campus you may be eligible for a 100%
appointment. If UCSF is your home campus, 100% is not guaranteed and may need
extra HR approval. International students should check with the Berkeley
International Office either way.

Hiring runs through the GSR Appointment Request system, and **all** HR and CPH
paperwork has to be finished before you start work.

!!! warning "Tell the GSAO a semester ahead"
    If you want to teach, inform the GSAO **no later than the end of the semester
    before** the one you plan to teach in. See
    [curriculum](curriculum.md#teaching) for the first-time GSI requirements at
    UC Berkeley — there are four of them, and they take time.

## Fellowships

| Fellowship | Eligibility | Deadline |
|---|---|---|
| NSF GRFP | US citizens/PRs, years 1–2 | Typically October |
| NIH F31 | Varies | Multiple cycles per year |
| **CPH Student Leadership and Community Engagement Fellowship** | CPH students in **year 2 and beyond** | Applications each spring; notified late summer |
| Competitive CPH fellowships | Awarded at admission or later | Ask the GSAO |

The **CPH Student Leadership and Community Engagement Fellowship** is a one-year
appointment with a monthly stipend from August to May, supporting work that
strengthens belonging, student success and access to academic and professional
development within CPH. It's the obvious one to know about if you're already
active in the GSA.

For finding everything else, CPH encourages students to set up a **PIVOT
account**, and UC Berkeley publishes a Fellowships Handbook.

!!! tip "The GSA keeps successful applications"
    Students who've won fellowships often share their applications. Ask an
    officer — these are shared privately with current students.

## Reimbursements

The rule that matters most: **get approval in writing before you spend anything.**
Approvals are generally required in advance, and an expense without one may
simply not be reimbursable.

Reimbursable categories include lab instruments and supplies, office and general
supplies, books and course materials, computers and computer supplies, telephone,
library materials, training, conference and event registration, journals and
subscriptions, and memberships.

Purchases made for PIs or for programming at UC Berkeley go through the **UC
Berkeley Reimbursement System** — a password-protected site where you upload
receipts, invoices and approval documents, and it routes the approvals for you.

## Funding a student event

CPH has a **Graduate Student Community/Events Fund** with a set annual budget, for
students who want to run events or buy resources for the CPH student community.

The process, in order:

1. Talk through the idea with the GSAO first.
2. Complete the **Expense Request Form** and email it to Bianca Victorica
   (`biancav@berkeley.edu`).
3. **Wait for approval.** Then buy.
4. Keep every receipt and invoice — and for events, a guest list of attendees.
5. Submit for reimbursement with the documentation.

!!! danger "Approval first, no exceptions"
    Any expense claimed against this fund must have an approved Expense Request
    Form *before* the money is spent. The handbook says this explicitly and says
    there are no exceptions. CPH also can't guarantee that a given reimbursement
    will be permitted, so check eligibility before you commit to anything
    expensive.
